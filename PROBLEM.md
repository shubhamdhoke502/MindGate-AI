# Known Problems in MindGate AI

MindGate AI is currently under active development. The following issues have been observed during testing.

## 1. AI Hallucinations / Incorrect Answers
The AI sometimes generates incorrect answers that do not accurately reflect the information in the uploaded documents.

## 2. Empty Responses
Sometimes, the system returns an empty response instead of providing an answer.

## 3. Context Retrieval Failures
Sometimes, the system reports that the relevant context was not found, even when asking questions about uploaded documents.

## Help Wanted

Contributors are welcome to investigate these issues, identify their root causes, and propose reliable fixes.

**Important:** These are observed symptoms. Their underlying causes have not yet been confirmed.
